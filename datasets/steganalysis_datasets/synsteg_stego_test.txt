The digital divide refers to the gap between those who have access to technology and those who do not
 It is a significant issue in today's society as technology plays a crucial role in education, communication, and economic opportunities
 Access to technology enables individuals to acquire new skills, stay connected, and access resources that can enhance their lives
 However, the digital divide perpetuates existing inequalities, preventing certain groups from benefiting fully from the digital revolution
 Bridging the gap requires initiatives that focus on providing affordable and reliable internet access, educational resources, and digital literacy training to ensure equal opportunities for all
